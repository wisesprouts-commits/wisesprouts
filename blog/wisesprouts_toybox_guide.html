The Toy Box Guide

A toy box
with no ceilings.

A free, science-informed guide to choosing toys by developmental skill — not gender. For parents raising kids ages 2 to 8.

7 min read
Ages 2–8
WiseSprouts Guides

Most toy aisles are still sorted by gender — pink on one side, blue on the other. But child development research doesn't sort that way. Every toy builds a specific skill: spatial reasoning, language, fine motor control, empathy, risk assessment. None of those skills belong to one gender.

This guide flips the sorting. Instead of "toys for girls" and "toys for boys," you'll find toys organized by what they actually build — so you can choose based on what your child needs, not what aisle they're standing in.

The question was never "is this toy appropriate for my child's gender." It was always "is this toy good for my child's growing brain."

⚠️ A note on real differences

Some average differences in movement and sensory needs between boys and girls are real and backed by research. But averages describe groups, not the child in front of you. The goal isn't to ignore temperament — it's to watch your actual child instead of pre-sorting by gender before you've had the chance to notice who they are.
