Why WiseSprouts
Parenting advice is everywhere. Support is not.

Most Indian parents are navigating parenting between two worlds — the values they grew up with, and the world their children are growing up in. Add joint families, academic pressure, screen time battles, and the endless scroll of conflicting advice online. It's exhausting.

WiseSprouts exists to simplify this. Every insight I share is grounded in child development research and filtered through an India-aware lens. No guilt. No extremes. No one-size-fits-all answers.

When you work with me, you're not getting a chatbot or a generic programme. You're getting a real conversation about your real child — their age, their temperament, your family, your context.
